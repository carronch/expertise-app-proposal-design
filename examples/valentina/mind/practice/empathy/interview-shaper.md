# Interview: shaper, 2026-09-19

## Why them

He does most of the repairs around here.

## What I asked

- What's the most common repair you see?
- How long does a small ding take you?

## What they said

1. "Soft-tops are easy."
2. "Half my epoxy jobs, somebody already fixed it with polyester from the hardware store. Now I fix it twice."
3. "A small ding is under an hour, but I have a line."

## What surprised me

The bad repairs cause more work than the dings.
