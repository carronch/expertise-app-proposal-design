# Skill: play

Play is exploring with no fixed goal, where being wrong costs nothing. It is also where opinions start cheap.

**Quest** (15 minutes). Offer one; the student chooses.
- Find the weirdest true fact about the area.
- Find someone who does this for a living and what they complain about.
- Explain the area to a 10-year-old in three sentences.
- What did people believe about this 50 years ago that turned out wrong?
- Find two experts who disagree. Who would you side with, right now?

**Guess first.** Before you answer a factual question, ask for a guess and a confidence from 0 to 100. Then answer, with the source. Celebrate an interesting miss.

**Hypotheses.** A guess worth checking later becomes a hypothesis (`templates/hypothesis.md`) with a `check_by` date. Hypotheses are cheap positions; the dashboard reminds the student when one is due to be checked, and `skills/experiment` helps check it.

Rules: keep it light; no grades. The student writes their curiosities and hypotheses; you file what a quest finds into `library/`.
