# Example: Valentina's vault

**Valentina is fictional.** She is 15, lives in Tamarindo, Costa Rica, and picked *surfboard repair for surf schools* as her focus area. The facts in `library/` that cite Wikipedia are real and checked. Her interviews, the numbers people gave her, and her experiment's results are **invented** to show how the vault works.

What to look at:

1. `_dashboard.md` — generated on 2026-10-03 with `python3 scripts/expertise.py dashboard examples/valentina --today 2026-10-03`.
2. The climb on one topic: a fact (`library/sources/wikipedia-surfboard.md`) → an insight in her words (`mind/insights/epoxy-boards-need-epoxy-repairs.md`) → a creation that teaches it (`mind/practice/creation/tiktok-epoxy-needs-epoxy.md`).
3. A position that met reality: `mind/positions/surf-schools-lose-more-to-waiting-than-to-damage.md`, the hypothesis it grew from, its interview and its experiment.
4. What the dashboard flags: a hypothesis past its check date, an insight with only one link, a position with no "what would change my mind", no play in two weeks.
