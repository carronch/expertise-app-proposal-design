# Experiment: will schools pay for repairs back in 48 hours?

## I expect

At least two of three schools will try it, and they'll care more about speed than price.

## The test

For one week, offer three schools: simple dings back in 48 hours, done by the shaper after hours, I pick up and deliver. Count boards and ask what mattered.

## Result

1. Two schools said yes; one said no because it keeps spare boards.
2. Seven boards, all back within 48 hours.
3. One owner: "The speed is what I'd pay for."

## What changed

My position went from 60 to 70. But the school with spare boards is the case that could prove me wrong, so I need more schools.
