# Surfboard (Wikipedia)

**What it is:** the English Wikipedia article on surfboards, read 2026-10-03. A general reference; good for basics, not for repair technique.

## Facts

- Modern boards are usually foam: polyurethane (PU) foam with polyester resin "have been used in surfboards since the 1950s". (section Construction)
- Polystyrene (PS) foam with epoxy resin "has a lower weight". (Construction)
- Epoxy is used instead of polyester on polystyrene boards because "the polystyrene foam reacts chemically upon contact with polyester resin, causing the PS foam to melt." (Construction)
- "Delamination is a problem where the skin of the board (i.e. fiberglass) separates from the foam core." (Construction)
