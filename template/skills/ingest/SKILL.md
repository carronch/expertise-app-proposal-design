# Skill: ingest

1. Read or watch the whole source. If you only saw part of it, say so under `## What I did not read`.
2. Write `library/sources/<slug>.md` from `templates/source.md`: what it is, 3 to 10 facts with locators, open questions. Facts are what the source supports, not your opinion and not the student's.
3. Interviews and experiments: the source is the student's note in `mind/`. Set `from: "[[their-note]]"` and quote them faithfully.
4. Link the source from its topic page(s) and from any people pages. Search `library/` before creating a new topic or person page.
5. Ask the student one question that connects the new facts to something they already wrote ("Does this agree with your hypothesis on X?"). That question is the seed of an insight. They write it.
