# Skill: quiz

1. Pick five to ten items: facts that the student's insights and positions link to (those matter most), plus one or two of their own insights ("Explain your insight on X without looking").
2. Ask one at a time. The student answers from memory; then show the source.
3. Spacing: a missed item comes back next session; a remembered item comes back after a longer gap (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month). Keep the schedule in `library/_quiz-state.md` (you write it).
4. No scores in `mind/`. A miss is information, not a grade.
