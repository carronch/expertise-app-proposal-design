# Clark Foam (Wikipedia)

**What it is:** the English Wikipedia article on Clark Foam, read 2026-10-03.

## Facts

- Clark Foam made surfboard blanks: "foam slabs, reinforced with one or more wooden strips or 'stringers'". (lead)
- It "provided about 90% of the United States supply and 60% of the world supply of surfboard blanks". (lead)
- Founded in 1961 by Gordon "Grubby" Clark. (lead)
- "On December 5, 2005, Gordon Clark abruptly shut down Clark Foam", which "left surfboard shapers scrambling for new suppliers". (Closure)
- Afterwards, manufacturing "turned to new and innovative materials, such as carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer, hollow blanks, and new 'flex' materials". (Aftermath)
