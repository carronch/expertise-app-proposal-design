# Surf schools lose more money to boards waiting for repair than to the damage itself

## I believe

For a school renting boards in high season, a board out of use for a week costs more in lost lessons than the repair costs. Most owners think about the repair bill, not the waiting.

## Because

- [[waiting-is-the-real-cost]] — repairs are fast, waiting is slow.
- [[interview-surf-school-owner-2026-09-12]] — five or six boards out, lessons turned away.
- [[experiment-48h-repair-pilot]] — the school cared more about turnaround than price.

## What would change my mind

If most schools keep enough spare boards that a board waiting for repair costs them no lessons. One school already said it does, so I need to ask at least five.
