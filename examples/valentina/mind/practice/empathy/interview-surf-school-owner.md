# Interview: surf school owner, 2026-09-12

## Why them

They own 40 boards and see dings every day.

## What I asked

- Tell me about the last board that got damaged. What happened next?
- How long was it out of the water?
- What did that cost you?

## What they said

1. "We have like forty boards, most are soft-tops."
2. "Every week five or six are waiting to be fixed."
3. "It takes one to two weeks, the shaper is always busy."
4. "In December we said no to two group lessons because we didn't have boards."

## What surprised me

They never mentioned the repair price. Only the waiting.
