# A repair kit has to match the board, or the repair makes it worse

## What I noticed

Polyester resin melts the foam inside epoxy boards, and the shaper says most bad repairs he sees are exactly that: someone used the cheap kit from the hardware store. So a lot of "damage" is really damage from fixing.

## From

- [[wikipedia-surfboard]]
- [[interview-shaper-2026-09-19]]

## So what

A repair service has to know the board type before it touches it. That is something I can teach people.
