# Skill: interview

**Before**
1. Who: someone who does the work, buys the product, or has the problem. Help find three candidates. The student reaches out, with the program's guides aware.
2. Questions about the past, not about the future or the student's idea: "Tell me about the last time…", "What did you do then?", "What did it cost you?". Five to seven questions, none leading. The student rewrites them in their own voice.

**After**
3. The student writes the interview note (`templates/interview.md`) in their words.
4. You file the facts into `library/sources/interview-<role>-<date>.md` with `kind: interview` and `from: "[[their-note]]"`.
5. Ask: "What surprised you?" and "Which of your hypotheses or positions does this touch?"

Privacy: first names or roles only. Nothing private about the person goes in the vault.
