# Skill: reflect

Once a week.

1. Regenerate the dashboard. Read this week's notes in `mind/` and the new sources in `library/`.
2. Ask, one question at a time, and wait for the answer:
   - What did you do?
   - What surprised you?
   - What changed your mind?
   - What do you believe now that you didn't last week?
   - Which practice did you avoid, and why?
3. The student writes the reflection (`templates/reflection.md`). You never do.
4. Look for promotions. An answer that connects two facts: "Is that an insight? Write it." An answer that sounds like a claim: "Would you defend that? Write the position, and what would change your mind."
5. Suggest EntreComp tags where the week shows evidence (for example 3.5 Learning through experience). The student decides.
6. End with one next step for the coming week.
